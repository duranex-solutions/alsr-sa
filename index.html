Services

What laser cleaning removes

An industrial-grade fibre laser lifts the unwanted layer off the surface and leaves the metal underneath intact. It works best on medium and large metal surfaces and structures.

Rust and corrosion removal

Takes surface rust and corrosion back to clean metal without grinding away the material beneath.

Typical jobs: vehicle chassis, trailers, structural steel, tanks

Paint and coating stripping

Strips light to medium paint and coatings. Multi-layer or thick coatings are assessed per job.

Typical jobs: vehicle parts, machinery, steelwork due for recoating

Oxide, mill scale and weld discolouration

Clears oxide layers, mill scale and heat tint around welds on steel and stainless steel.

Typical jobs: fabricated steel, stainless welds, new steel before coating

Oil, grease and carbon residue

Removes oil, grease and carbon build-up from metal without solvents or degreasers.

Typical jobs: machinery, moulds in service, mining and marine equipment

Surface prep for paint, coating or welding

Leaves a clean, lightly textured surface that helps new paint and coatings adhere.

Typical jobs: pre-paint prep, pre-weld cleaning, recoating projects

Laser cleaning vs sandblasting and chemical stripping

Sandblasting and chemical strippers both get the job done, but they leave you with waste, clean-up and wear on the part. Here is how the three methods compare.

Laser cleaning compared with sandblasting and chemical stripping
Factor Laser cleaning Sandblasting Chemical stripping
Waste No media. Only the removed rust, paint or residue. Spent grit and dust to contain and dispose of Used chemicals and sludge to dispose of
Effect on the base metal Non-abrasive. Removes the surface layer, not the metal. Abrasive. Wears away some base material. Can etch or attack some metals
Chemicals None None Solvents or caustic strippers
Clean-up Minimal Extensive. Grit gets into gaps, bearings and machinery. Neutralise, rinse and dispose
Targeting one area Cleans just the area you need Needs heavy masking Needs masking and dwell time
On your site Yes, fully mobile Usually needs containment or a blasting bay Needs ventilation and run-off control

Is laser cleaning right for my job?

It suits most rust, paint, scale and residue on medium and large metal surfaces: vehicle chassis and parts, machinery, structural steel, trailers, tanks, moulds in service, and marine and mining equipment. A few cases need a closer look:

  • Very thin sheet metal and delicate or precision parts can be affected by heat, so we assess these first and will tell you if laser cleaning isn't the right choice.
  • Very thick or built-up coatings take longer, so we quote them after an assessment.
  • Glass and highly reflective or polished surfaces are assessed case by case.
  • Plastics, rubber, composites, wood, electronics and heat-sensitive parts aren't suited to laser cleaning, and we'll point you elsewhere.

Not sure? We offer a free photo assessment or test patch.

Which areas do you cover, and is there a call-out fee?

We're based in Kempton Park and work across Gauteng and surrounding areas, with no call-out fee in Gauteng. We also travel nationwide for larger jobs. Outside Gauteng, a distance-based call-out fee may apply, and we'll include it in your quote.

Why don't you publish prices?

Every job is different. The price depends on the measured surface area and on how thick the rust, paint or contaminant is. Rather than give you a rough number that changes later, we quote each job properly from your photos or a test patch.

What do you need from us on site?

Very little. We bring all of our own equipment, including our own power, so you don’t need to supply anything.

The one thing we ask: somewhere to park within about 30–40 metres of where the work will be done. If that isn’t possible, tell us when you request your quote and we’ll plan around it.

What is the surface like after cleaning?

Clean and lightly textured, which helps new paint and coatings adhere. It's ready for painting, coating or welding. Bare steel starts to rust again when exposed to moisture, so plan to prime or coat it soon after cleaning.

Which materials can you clean?

Steel, stainless steel, cast iron, aluminium, copper and brass, and titanium. Some stone and concrete can be cleaned too, after an assessment.
